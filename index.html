My Top 3 Hawaii Foods

I grew up in Pearl City on Oahu, and a lot of my favorite memories back home involve food. Whether it was grabbing a plate lunch after practice or eating at a family party, local food was always a big part of life.

What makes food in Hawaii special is how many cultures it brings together. My top three picks show that mix: kalua pork from Native Hawaiian tradition, chicken katsu from Japan, and kalbi from Korea. Click any button above to learn about each one, where it came from, and where I like to get it.
